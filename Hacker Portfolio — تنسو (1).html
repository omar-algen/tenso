HACKER PROFILE
Background & Philosophy




I’m a self-driven ethical hacker who learns security by building labs, breaking them, and writing down exactly how and why they failed. I work only inside authorized scope.


My approach is simple: think like an attacker, report like an engineer. A vulnerability is only useful once it is reproduced, rated by real impact, and paired with a clear fix.


Understanding how systems break is the fastest way to build ones that don’t.
